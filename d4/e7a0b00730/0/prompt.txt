## Race assessment (honest)

The reviewer is right that the probe was TOCTOU, and the sticky flag made it worse than a transient: a loser that probed pre-creation kept `createdFresh=true` for its entire retry budget. The heal-under-lock ordering does close most interleavings — a live client's uncommitted writes require a *completed* init, after which `migrationWorkNeeded` short-circuits before the guard, so no heal can fire — but one path stays exploitable: **loser with a newer binary** (more known migrations) acquires the lock after the winner's init completed at cursor N and a client left uncommitted writes on `issues`; pending N+1..M touch `issues`, the guard fires, and the falsely-"fresh" loser resets real data. Narrow (needs concurrent init of the same absent DB + version skew + uncommitted client writes inside a 60s window) but real, and the hardening is cheap. Verdict: valid P1.
